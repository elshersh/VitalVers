01 / OUR PURPOSE

Our
Mission

Vitalverse is a personalized health monitoring platform for astronauts. Instead of judging readings against the same fixed limits for everyone, it compares each astronaut’s heart rate, oxygen, blood pressure, sleep and mood with their own pre-flight baseline.

When something changes, it explains what changed, how it compares with their normal, and what to do next. Astronauts get a clear, friendly view of their own health. Flight Surgeons get a crew overview, alerts to review and a day-by-day replay of the mission.

The medical team always makes the final decision.

Important Notice: Vitalverse is a prototype built with fictional data. It is not a certified medical device and does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
