# NASA HRP: Ongoing Research & Platforms

**Source:** [NASA Human Research Program (HRP)](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/ongoing-hrp-research/)

## CIPHER (Complement of Integrated Protocols for Human Exploration Research)
An integrated series of up to 14 physiological and psychological studies conducted on ISS astronauts on short, standard (6-month), and extended (1-year) missions.

**Core Investigations:**
- Cardiovascular remodeling and vascular stiffness
- Bone mineral density changes and biomechanical strength
- Ocular structure alterations and optic nerve sheath dilation (SANS)
- Immune system biomarkers and microbiome shifts
- Cognitive performance degradation and sleep architecture

## Standard Measures
A baseline data repository collecting a standardized set of physiological, behavioral, and biochemical data from every ISS astronaut before, during, and after flight.

**Standard Data Collected:**
- Biochemical: blood chemistry, hematology, urinalysis, endocrine hormones
- Physiological: body mass, exercise vitals, ECG, spirometry
- Sensorimotor: posture, dynamic balance, gait analysis
- Psychological: sleep diary, cognitive test batteries (Cognition test), stress surveys

## Ground-Based Analog Environments
Earth-based isolated habitats simulating microgravity, isolation, and communication latency to validate countermeasures prior to flight.

**Key Analog Facilities:**
- HERA (Human Exploration Research Analog) at NASA Johnson Space Center: 45-day simulated isolation campaigns testing crew dynamics, sleep debt, and autonomous operations.
- CHAPEA (Crew Health and Performance Exploration Analog): 3D-printed Mars habitat evaluating 1-year missions.
- :envihab & Bed-Rest Studies: -6 degree head-down tilt bed rest mimicking microgravity cephalad fluid shifts and bone/muscle disuse.
- Antarctic Research Stations: long polar winter confinement evaluating extreme cold isolation and circadian disruption.
