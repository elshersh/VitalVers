# NASA HRP: Risks of Human Spaceflight & Mitigation Framework

**Source:** [NASA Human Research Program (HRP)](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/)

## Element: Human Health Countermeasures (HHC)
**Focus Area:** Physiological adaptation, cardiovascular deconditioning, bone/muscle loss, neuro-vestibular shifts, immunology, and nutritional requirements.

### Risk of Spaceflight-Induced Bone Demineralization and Fractures
- **Primary Hazard:** Gravity Fields
- **Physiological Impact:** Astronauts lose ~1-1.5% bone mineral density per month in cancellous weight-bearing bones. Leads to elevated hypercalciuria, increased risk of renal stone formation, and early-onset osteoporosis.
- **Key Monitored Metrics:** DXA T/Z-scores, Serum/urinary calcium, Bone turnover markers (NTX, CTX)

### Risk of Impaired Muscle Performance and Muscle Atrophy
- **Primary Hazard:** Gravity Fields
- **Physiological Impact:** Preferential loss of muscle mass and power in leg extensors and postural muscles (soleus, gastrocnemius), impairing post-landing egress and EVA capability.
- **Key Monitored Metrics:** Isometric muscle strength (N), Calf circumference (cm), Daily resistive workload (kJ)

### Risk of Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome (SANS)
- **Primary Hazard:** Gravity Fields / Hostile Closed Environment
- **Physiological Impact:** Progressive ocular structural changes: optic disc swelling (papilledema), globe flattening, choroidal folds, and hyperopic refractive shift caused by cephalad fluid pressure.
- **Key Monitored Metrics:** Optic nerve sheath diameter (ultrasound mm), Retinal nerve fiber layer thickness (OCT), Visual acuity (Snellen / logMAR)

### Risk of Cardiovascular Disease and Orthostatic Intolerance
- **Primary Hazard:** Gravity Fields / Radiation
- **Physiological Impact:** Decreased cardiac left ventricular mass, arterial stiffening, baroreceptor reset, and reduced plasma volume causing presyncope upon return to gravitational fields.
- **Key Monitored Metrics:** Resting heart rate (bpm), Continuous blood pressure (mmHg), Heart rate variability (HRV)

### Risk of Adverse Health Consequences from Immune Dysregulation
- **Primary Hazard:** Hostile Closed Environment / Radiation / Isolation
- **Physiological Impact:** Decreased cell-mediated immunity, altered cytokine profiles, and subclinical viral reactivation (Epstein-Barr, Cytomegalovirus, Varicella Zoster) manifesting as skin rashes and hypersensitivity.
- **Key Monitored Metrics:** White blood cell differentials, Viral load shedding in saliva/urine, Body temperature (°C)

## Element: Human Factors and Behavioral Performance (HFBP)
**Focus Area:** Behavioral health, psychiatric stability, neurocognitive performance, sleep hygiene, team cohesion, and human-system integration.

### Risk of Adverse Cognitive or Behavioral Conditions and Psychiatric Disorders
- **Primary Hazard:** Isolation and Confinement
- **Physiological Impact:** Chronic confinement and lack of Earth contact induce emotional instability, anxiety, depressive symptoms, and declining mood.
- **Key Monitored Metrics:** Self-reported mood score (1-10), Visual analog depression/anxiety scales, Behavioral logs

### Risk of Performance Decrements and Errors Due to Sleep Loss and Circadian Desynchronization
- **Primary Hazard:** Isolation and Confinement / Hostile Environment
- **Physiological Impact:** Acute or chronic sleep deprivation impairs attention, executive judgment, spatial reasoning, and motor performance during safety-critical operations.
- **Key Monitored Metrics:** Sleep duration (hours/24hr), Sleep efficiency (%), Cognitive reaction time (ms via PVT test)

### Risk of Inadequate Team Function, Communication, and Interpersonal Friction
- **Primary Hazard:** Isolation and Confinement / Distance from Earth
- **Physiological Impact:** Interpersonal conflicts between crew members or between crew and mission control can degrade operational workflow and compromise mission safety.
- **Key Monitored Metrics:** Team communication sentiment index, Sociometric interaction logs, Task completion latency

## Element: Exploration Medical Capabilities (ExMC)
**Focus Area:** Autonomous clinical medicine, medical decision support, point-of-care laboratory diagnostics, and medical inventory optimization.

### Risk of Inability to Provide Adequate Medical Care in Deep Space
- **Primary Hazard:** Distance from Earth
- **Physiological Impact:** Communication latency prevents real-time tele-medical guidance; limited onboard clinical volume and mass restricts available therapies for trauma, acute abdomen, or sepsis.
- **Key Monitored Metrics:** Clinical checklist adherence, Diagnostic accuracy scores, Medical consumable usage rates

## Element: Space Radiation Element (SRE)
**Focus Area:** Quantifying and mitigating radiation biological effects, DNA repair mechanisms, and permissible exposure guidelines.

### Risk of Radiation Carcinogenesis and Acute Radiation Syndromes
- **Primary Hazard:** Space Radiation
- **Physiological Impact:** GCR and SPE ionization breaks double-strand DNA, elevating lifetime excess risk of cancer mortality and tissue degeneration.
- **Key Monitored Metrics:** Cumulative absorbed dose (mGy), Cumulative equivalent dose (mSv), Cytogenetic chromosome aberration frequency
