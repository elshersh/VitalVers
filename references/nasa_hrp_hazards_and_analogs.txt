# NASA Human Research Program (HRP) Hazard Pages, Risks, and Analog Environments

SOURCE_URL: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/
WAYBACK_SNAPSHOT_DATE: 2026-07-10 to 2026-11-09

## 1. NASA HRP — Hazard: Gravity Fields
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-gravity-fields/
SECTION: Bone, Cardiovascular, and Fluid Shift Overview
"Weight-bearing bones lose on average 1% to 1.5% of mineral density per month during spaceflight."
"The trip between Earth and Mars is described as six months of weightlessness; Mars has about one-third of Earth's gravity."
"Fluids shift upward to the head, which may put pressure on the eyes and cause vision problems."

## 2. NASA HRP — Hazard: Isolation and Confinement
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-isolation-and-confinement/
SECTION: Behavioral Health, Analogs, and Circadian Research
"Sleep loss, circadian desynchronization and work overload may lead to decreased performance and adverse health outcomes."
"HERA analog: crews live inside for 45 days at a time. CHAPEA: year-long simulated Mars stays."
"Related items mention journaling and space gardening as coping tools, alongside light therapy for circadian alignment."

## 3. NASA HRP — Hazard: Hostile Closed Environments
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-hostile-closed-environments/
SECTION: Immune and Microbial Environment
"Stress hormone levels can elevate and the immune system can alter, which could lead to increased susceptibility to allergies or other illnesses."
"Microbes transfer more easily between people in closed habitats, requiring continuous air revitalization, HEPA filtration, and surface disinfection."

## 4. NASA HRP — Hazard: Distance from Earth
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazard-distance-from-earth/
SECTION: Communication Latency and Autonomous Medical Care
"Mars lies an average of 140 million miles from Earth (Moon ~240,000 miles), creating communication delays of up to 20 to 22 minutes each way."

## 5. NASA HRP — Ongoing Research: CIPHER, Standard Measures, and Bed Rest
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/ongoing-hrp-research/
SECTION: CIPHER & Standard Measures Overview
"CIPHER integrates up to 14 physiological and psychological studies across short, standard 6-month, and extended 1-year spaceflight missions."
"Head-down tilt bed rest at -6 degrees simulates microgravity cephalad fluid shifts and musculoskeletal disuse on Earth."
