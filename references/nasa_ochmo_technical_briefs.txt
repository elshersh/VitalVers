# NASA Office of the Chief Health and Medical Officer (OCHMO) Technical Briefs

SOURCE_URL: https://www.nasa.gov/ochmo/health-and-medical-policy/nasa-std-3001-technical-briefs/

## OCHMO-TB-030: Bone Mineral Loss (2023)
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/ochmo-tb-030-bone-mineral-loss.pdf
SECTION: Overview & Countermeasures (Pages 1-3)
"Without gravitational loading, weight-bearing bones lose 1% to 1.5% of bone mineral density per month in spaceflight, increasing renal stone and fracture risk."
"High-intensity resistive exercise on the Advanced Resistive Exercise Device (ARED), adequate vitamin D and calcium intake, and bisphosphonate therapy protect bone mineral density."

## OCHMO-TB-031: Exercise Countermeasures Overview (2023)
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/ochmo-tb-031-exercise-overview.pdf
SECTION: ISS Exercise Prescription & Hardware (Pages 1-4)
"Astronauts on the ISS are allocated approximately 2.0 to 2.5 hours per day for combined resistive (ARED) and aerobic (T2 treadmill, CEVIS) exercise."
"Daily combined resistive and aerobic exercise mitigates skeletal muscle atrophy, bone mineral density loss, and cardiovascular deconditioning during microgravity missions."

## OCHMO-TB-033: Spaceflight Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome (SANS) (2023)
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/ochmo-tb-033-sans.pdf
SECTION: Cephalad Fluid Shift & Ocular Monitoring (Pages 1-4)
"In microgravity, approximately 2 liters of intravascular and interstitial fluid shift cephalad toward the head and thorax, contributing to optic disc edema and globe flattening."
"Ocular ultrasound measurement of optic nerve sheath diameter (ONSD) 3 mm posterior to the globe monitors intracranial fluid shifts; Lower Body Negative Pressure (LBNP) is investigated as a mechanical countermeasure."

## OCHMO-TB-015: Space Motion Sickness and Sensorimotor Adaptation (2023)
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/ochmo-tb-015-sensorimotor.pdf
SECTION: Time Course & Compression Garments (Pages 1-3)
"Vestibular disorientation and space motion sickness typically occur during the initial 48 to 72 hours of weightlessness and upon return to gravity."
"Pre-landing fluid and electrolyte loading combined with lower-body compression garments mitigate post-flight orthostatic intolerance and presyncope."

## OCHMO-TB-004: Carbon Dioxide (CO2) and Habitat Air Quality (2023)
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/ochmo-tb-004-carbon-dioxide.pdf
SECTION: Clinical Symptoms & ECLSS Scrubbing (Pages 1-3)
"Elevated cabin carbon dioxide partial pressures above 3 to 5 mmHg increase incidence of crew headaches, lethargy, and neurobehavioral impairment."

## OCHMO-TB-025: Behavioral Health, Sleep, and Circadian Rhythm (2023)
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/ochmo-tb-025-sleep-and-circadian.pdf
SECTION: Behavioral Countermeasures (Pages 1-4)
"Structured sleep-wake schedules, circadian-aligned dynamic lighting, private psychological conferences, and family communication mitigate behavioral health decline during isolation and confinement."

## OCHMO-TB-038: Immune System Dysregulation in Spaceflight (2023)
URL: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/ochmo-tb-038-immune-system.pdf
SECTION: Immune Countermeasures & Clinical Monitoring (Pages 1-3)
"Spaceflight immune dysregulation involves altered T-cell function and latent herpesvirus reactivation; countermeasures include stress reduction, adequate sleep, nutrition, and exercise."
